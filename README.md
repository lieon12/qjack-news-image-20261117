# news-image-20261117 — The World in One Day

One image summing up Tuesday 17 November 2026's world news: a globe at the
centre, ringed by the day's five biggest stories, each with a short label,
under a masthead naming the date. The stories pictured are (1) Ukraine's
strike on the Kerch Bridge to Crimea, a section falling into the strait;
(2) the US strike on an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island after Iran
fired drones at a US radar in Bahrain; (3) Trump, Xi and Putin meeting for
trilateral talks at APEC in Shenzhen; (4) Nvidia's record quarterly revenue
of more than $115 billion, with ousted CEO Jensen Huang not reinstated;
(5) the United States losing its measles elimination status after 26 years,
with more than 5,400 cases reported. `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard
library only) writes the embedded image byte for byte to
`/output/entry.png`.
